Definitions and thresholds

Name the level before you respond

Event

Observable occurrence

A failed login on the LIMS

Incident

Violates policy or threatens operations

Malware found on an instrument PC

Breach

Confirmed loss of data

Client results copied out

Crisis

Threatens safety or the institution

Freezers or containment affected

Four levels, one click each. Most alerts are events. Calling every event a crisis exhausts the team; calling a crisis an event delays the response. Write the thresholds down before you need them. In a laboratory, anything that touches containment or sample viability goes straight to the top level.
