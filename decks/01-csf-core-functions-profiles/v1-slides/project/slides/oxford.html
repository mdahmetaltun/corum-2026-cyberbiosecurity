February 2021 · University of Oxford · Structural Biology

Intruders reached the controls of equipment that prepares biochemical samples

Exposed

Pumps and pressure controls

Screenshots shared with Forbes showed live access to instrument interfaces.

Context

A lab doing COVID-19 research

Timestamps suggested access as recently as 14 February.

Response

Contained and reported

The university notified the NCSC; it said no clinical research or patient data were affected.

The lesson

Instrument controllers are IT assets. Nobody had them on an inventory, so nobody was protecting them.

ESET WeLiveSecurity, 26 Feb 2021, reporting Forbes and Hold Security

A real case close to home for everyone in this room. In February 2021 Oxford confirmed that attackers had accessed systems in its Structural Biology division, including the controls of machines that purify and prepare biochemical samples. The point is not that the attackers were sophisticated; reports suggest they were not. The point is that laboratory equipment was reachable, and nobody had treated it as an IT asset.
