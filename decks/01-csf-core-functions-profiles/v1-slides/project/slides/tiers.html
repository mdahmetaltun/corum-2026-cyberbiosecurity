Tiers

How rigorous are our practices?

1

Partial

Managed ad hoc; limited awareness across the organization.

2

Risk Informed

Approved by management, not yet organization-wide policy.

3

Repeatable

Formally approved, expressed as policy, regularly updated.

4

Adaptive

Adapts from lessons learned and predictive indicators.

Tiers are not a score to maximise. Choose the tier your risk justifies; a teaching lab and a BSL-3 core facility need different targets.

Tiers describe the rigour of your practices, from ad hoc to adaptive. They apply to governance and to management of risk. Stress the sentence at the top: higher is not automatically better. Tier 3, outlined in orange, is a sensible target for many research facilities; Tier 4 costs real money and staff.
