Quick history. Version 1.0 came out in 2014 for critical infrastructure. Version 1.1 in 2018 kept the five functions and added supply-chain and identity material. Version 2.0, published 26 February 2024, is the version we teach today. Two headline changes: the audience is now every organization, and governance became its own function.
