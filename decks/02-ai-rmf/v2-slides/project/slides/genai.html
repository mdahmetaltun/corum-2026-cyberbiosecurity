NIST AI RMF · Research Laboratories · Çorum 2026

15

The Generative AI Profile lists twelve risks; they pop in one by one. The eight with orange tops matter most to us: CBRN information, confabulation, data privacy, human-AI configuration (over-reliance), information integrity, information security, intellectual property, and the value chain, meaning the third-party models and plug-ins you build on. The first one, CBRN information or capabilities, is the one this workshop exists for.
