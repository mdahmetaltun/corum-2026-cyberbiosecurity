Urbina and colleagues, 2022. Their company builds machine-learning models that predict toxicity so that drug candidates avoid it. For a biosecurity conference they inverted one objective. In under six hours the model proposed 40,000 molecules scoring as highly toxic, including VX. They synthesised nothing, and they deliberately withheld details. The lesson for us is not about chemistry: a capability built for good can be repurposed by changing one line, and nobody in the lab had thought about it before.
