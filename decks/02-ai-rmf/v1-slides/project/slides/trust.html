The figure builds as NIST draws it. First the foundation, valid and reliable: a model that is wrong is not made trustworthy by anything else. Then the five pillars. Then accountability and transparency, which run alongside all of them. Example of a trade-off: privacy enhancement can reduce accuracy, and explainability can cost performance. The framework asks you to make those trade-offs consciously.
